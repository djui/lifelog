How it works

A mirror forgets. An archive doesn't.

Messages expire, notes get deleted, events move. Lifelog reads your data on a schedule, records every change in an append-only ledger, and writes pages you can read with anything.

Nothing is dropped

Deleted, expired and unsent items stay where they were, struck through and marked with what happened, plus a monthly _Disappeared digest.

Plain files, forever

Markdown pages by source and by month, and a Timeline across all of them. Open them in any editor, or give the folder to the AI tool you choose.

Offline viewer

A static HTML viewer with full-text search, coverage per source and a timeline. No server, no network: double-click index.html.

Lives in the menu bar

Archives every 10 minutes (and hourly for Notes and AI exports), catches up after sleep, and shows each source's last read at a glance.

Read-only by design

Lifelog never changes your Apple or WhatsApp data. It only reads, and only writes to the folder you pick.

A CLI underneath

The same binary runs lifelog archive, rebuild, verify and open from Terminal or launchd.

Careful by default

Absence is not deletion.

A flaky read should never rewrite your history. An item only counts as gone when all four of these hold.

The read was completeTimeouts, permission errors and partial reads only ever add and update.
The item was in scopeOnly items the read would have covered can go missing.
It was missing twiceThe first miss is pending and invisible; the second consecutive miss makes it final.
The circuit breaker heldIf over 20% of a source vanishes at once, the read is suspected, not your data.

Install

Up and running in a minute.

Download the app, or build it yourself. There are no third-party dependencies.

  1. 1. Download and move to ApplicationsGet the latest release, unzip it and drag Lifelog.app into /Applications.
  2. 2. Open itDouble-click it. Lifelog is signed and notarized by Apple, and lives in your menu bar, not the Dock.
  3. 3. Pick a folderChoose an empty archive folder in the menu and click Start Archiving Here…. Turn on Open at Login.
  4. 4. Grant access onceAllow Calendar, Reminders, Notes automation and Full Disk Access (for WhatsApp) when asked.
Terminal
# or build from source (Swift 5.9+)
$ git clone https://github.com/djui/lifelog
$ cd lifelog && ./scripts/build.sh

# try it with synthetic data first
$ ./dist/lifelog archive --output ./demo --demo
$ ./dist/lifelog open --output ./demo

# the real thing
$ ./dist/lifelog archive --output ~/Archive

A word on privacy

Lifelog keeps other people's WhatsApp messages after they expired or were unsent, in plain text, in the folder you choose. Those people chose to make them disappear; your archive keeps your copy anyway.

There's no extra encryption: the archive relies on FileVault and wherever you keep it. Choose that place, and who you share it with, accordingly.
